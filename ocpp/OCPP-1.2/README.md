# OCPP 1.2

Historical copy of the OCPP 1.2 specification (2010) and its two WSDL files,
superseded for over a decade. The files are kept unmodified, with their
notices, under the repository's
[archival policy](../../docs/third-party-assets.md#archival-policy); see
[the third-party assets inventory](../../docs/third-party-assets.md#ocpp-ocpp)
for the rights holders of each file.
